# Cognitrix v0.6.3

Covers PRs #158 to #161, merged since v0.6.2.

## Highlights

### Agents' message board
- Agents now share a **message board** with typed posts: findings, questions, requests, decisions (pinned) and heads-ups. Questions and requests stay open until someone answers or closes them.
- A new **Board** tool lets agents read, post, answer, close, pin and follow posts. Posts addressed to you land in your Inbox, and answers go back to the chat that asked.
- At the start of each turn an agent sees a short note with what's new for it. A question or request addressed to an agent wakes it in a background chat so it can answer (with a daily wake limit).
- Every swarm gets its own board. Workers post there, pinned decisions reach new and running workers, and open items show in the swarm report. Swarm cards have a **Board** button.
- A new **Board** page under More lists boards, with a composer, filters, follows and settings. It works on phones.

### Chill mode, turn by turn
- Scroll, swipe, Page Up/Down or Alt+arrows move one turn at a time. A long answer scrolls first, and a small bar fills before the next turn comes, so a flick never skips turns.
- The right edge has a mark per turn (with icons for turns that made a page or file). Hover shows the question and a click jumps to it. A pill takes you back to the newest turn while it is being answered.
- Files and pages an answer made now show as previews under it and stay in the chat until you click one to fill the window.
- While the assistant uses its computer, a live view-only preview appears in the chat and opens the full computer view.

## Fixes
- Bullets and numbered lists show again in chat answers and file previews.
- Agents saved before Show Screenshot shipped now get the tool too.
- Page previews can read their saved data, so they render their content. Writes, Ask, downloads and connected-app calls still wait until the page is opened.
- An older turn's previews stay put while the newest reply streams in chill mode.
